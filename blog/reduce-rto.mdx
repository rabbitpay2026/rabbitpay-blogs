## Why RTO hurts D2C brands

Return-To-Origin (RTO) eats into margins through reverse logistics, damaged inventory, and lost working capital. For most Indian Shopify brands, COD orders are the biggest RTO contributor.

## 5 strategies that actually move the needle

### 1. Verify shoppers with OTP at checkout

Phone OTP filters out fake numbers and bots before the order is placed, cutting a large share of undeliverable COD orders.

### 2. Ask for a partial prepayment on COD

Collecting even 10 to 20 percent upfront dramatically increases the shopper's intent to accept delivery.

### 3. Validate addresses in real time

Autocomplete and pincode checks catch typos, missing landmarks, and non-serviceable zones before the order ships.

### 4. Suppress COD on risky orders

Use order value, past RTO history, and delivery pincode risk to hide COD selectively rather than blanket-disabling it.

### 5. Confirm high-value COD orders

A quick confirmation nudge (WhatsApp or IVR) on high-value COD orders reduces buyer's remorse cancellations.

## Putting it together

RTO reduction is rarely one lever. Stacking verification, partial payments, and smarter COD rules typically brings RTO down by 20 to 40 percent for growing Shopify brands.
