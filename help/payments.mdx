RabbitPay collects prepaid and partial payments through **your own** payment gateway account, so the money settles straight to you. Supported gateways:

| Gateway | What it offers at checkout | Keys you need |
|---|---|---|
| **Razorpay** | UPI, cards, netbanking and more | **Key ID** and **Key secret** |
| **PayU** | Hosted and Bolt checkout for UPI, cards, netbanking, EMI and wallets | **Merchant Key** and **Salt** |
| **Cashfree Payments** | UPI, cards, netbanking and wallets | **App ID** and **Secret Key** |

## Connect a gateway

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your keys">
    Sign in to your gateway's dashboard and copy the keys listed above. Use **live** keys for real orders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Payments">
    In the RabbitPay dashboard, go to **Settings → Payments** and click **Configure** on your gateway.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the keys">
    Paste your keys. For **PayU** and **Cashfree**, also choose the **Environment**:
    - **Test** (`test.payu.in` / `sandbox.cashfree.com`) for test keys
    - **Production** (`secure.payu.in` / `api.cashfree.com`) for live keys

    Test keys only work with the Test environment. If they don't match, the gateway rejects the payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up the webhook">
    In your gateway's dashboard, add the RabbitPay webhook so we're told when a payment succeeds, even if the shopper closes the page. Then tick **I have successfully configured the webhook**. Our team will share the webhook URL with you during onboarding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and enable">
    Save. The gateway card now shows a switch. Turn it to **Enabled** to start taking payments through it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Only one gateway is active at a time

Checkout can only send payments to one gateway. When you enable a gateway, any other enabled gateway is switched off automatically, and the message tells you which one. Turning a gateway off does **not** switch another one on. If you turn your only gateway off, prepaid payments stop until you enable one again.

## Update or remove a gateway

- **Change keys:** open the gateway's settings again and enter the new keys. You don't need to tick the webhook box again if it's already set up.
- **Disconnect:** click **Disconnect**. RabbitPay warns you first, because this removes the saved keys and related payment settings from your store. If you also linked the gateway in Shopify, unlink it there too.

## Show or hide payment methods

To choose which methods (for example UPI or cards) appear on the payment step, go to **Customization**. To offer different methods for different carts, use payment rules in the [RTO Suite](/help/rto-suite).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Shoppers can't pay online">
    Check that your gateway shows **Enabled** in **Settings → Payments**. For PayU or Cashfree, check that the environment matches your keys (live keys need **Production**).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Payment succeeded but the order is missing">
    This usually means the webhook isn't set up. Add it in your gateway's dashboard, then contact support with the payment ID so we can check the order.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I enabled a second gateway and the first one stopped">
    That's expected: only one gateway can be active. Enable the one you want to use.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
