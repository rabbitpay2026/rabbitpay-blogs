The **RTO Suite** is where you control how shoppers can pay. It has two parts:

- **Payment rules**: the payment options shown at checkout (full prepaid, partial payment upfront, COD), and which carts see each one.
- **Return-risk engine**: scores each checkout for return-to-origin (RTO) risk and can grey out COD for high-risk shoppers.

## Payment rules

Each rule is one payment option at checkout, for example "Pay 20% now, rest on delivery" or "Cash on Delivery". The list shows each rule's **Order**, **Rule name** and **Payment amount**.

Rules are checked **in order, top to bottom**, so put your preferred default first. Use the up and down arrows to reorder them.

### Add a rule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open RTO Suite">
    Go to **RTO Suite** and click **Add payment rule**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the rule and set the amount">
    The **Rule name** and amount are shown to shoppers at checkout. Pick an **Amount type**:

    | Amount type | What the shopper pays upfront |
    |---|---|
    | **Percentage** | A share of the order, e.g. 20% now and the rest on delivery |
    | **Fixed amount** | A fixed sum now, e.g. ₹199, and the rest on delivery |
    | **Full payment** | 100% prepaid |
    | **COD** | Nothing upfront. Pays on delivery, optionally with a COD fee. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when the rule applies">
    Narrow the rule down with the options in the next section, such as cart range, products, collections, pincodes or phone numbers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Make sure **Rule enabled** is on, then click **Create rule**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Rule options

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Cart range">
    Show the rule only when the cart total is between **Min cart total** and **Max cart total**. Leave a side blank for no limit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Description">
    A **Custom description** shown under the payment option. Leave it blank to use the default wording. Each line shows on its own line.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Partial COD fee">
    For partial-payment rules, turn on **Include COD fee on delivery** to add a fee to the amount paid on delivery. Choose how it's calculated:
    - **Fixed amount** (₹)
    - **Percentage of order value**, charged on the order total including tax and shipping
    - **Whichever is higher**, e.g. ₹199 or 2% of the order value, whichever is more

    If the fee is blank or 0, no COD fee is added.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Discount">
    Reward shoppers who choose this option with a discount: a **Discount code**, a **Discount type** (fixed or percentage), and a **Discount value**. **Apply discount on** can be:
    - **Total amount (once per order)**
    - **Per product (× cart quantity)**: a ₹100 discount becomes ₹200 for 2 items, ₹300 for 3, and so on.

    Under **Applicable discounts on COD**, choose whether **Manual discounts** (codes and gift coupons the shopper enters) and **Automatic discounts (Shopify)** still apply when the shopper picks COD. When off, COD is charged the full amount without that discount. Prepaid isn't affected.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Applies to (products)">
    - **All products**: every item in the cart.
    - **Only selected**: the cart must include one of the listed products.
    - **All except selected**: hidden when the cart only contains the excluded products.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Collections">
    - **All collections**: no collection restriction.
    - Show only when every cart product is in a selected collection.
    - **Hide for selected**: hidden when any cart product is in a selected collection.

    Products added to a selected collection later are covered automatically.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Payment methods">
    Choose which prepaid methods (such as **UPI** and **Cards**) show at checkout for this rule. All are on by default.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="COD availability">
    - **Show COD as disabled when blocked**: when COD is blocked for a product or pincode, show it greyed out with your message instead of hiding it. The default message is "Cash on Delivery isn't available for this item or in your area".
    - **Max items for COD**: when the cart has more items than this (total quantity), COD is greyed out and only prepaid is available. Leave it blank for no limit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pincode (zipcode) restrictions">
    - **Enable zipcode restrictions**: hide the rule for the pincodes you list.
    - **Enable zipcode whitelisting**: show the rule only for the pincodes you list.

    Separate pincodes with commas or new lines. Ranges like `400001-400010` work when both ends have the same number of digits. If both lists are on, a pincode must be on the whitelist **and** not on the blocked list. This needs address collection at checkout.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Phone number restrictions">
    Hide the rule for listed phone numbers, or show it only for listed numbers. Numbers are matched on the shopper's **verified** phone number using the last 10 digits, so `+91 98765 43210` and `9876543210` count as the same number.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Edit, disable or delete a rule

Click the pencil icon to edit a rule, or turn **Rule enabled** off to hide it without deleting it. Click the delete icon to remove a rule for good.

## Return-risk engine

The **Return-risk engine** scores every checkout for RTO risk, using the shopper's pincode and order history. It's **off by default**.

When it's on, every checkout that reaches the address step is scored, and the verdict shows on the order and on the abandoned-cart lead.

### Grey out COD for high-risk customers

With the engine on, you can also turn on **Grey out COD for high-risk customers**. High-risk shoppers still see COD with a short explanation, and can pay online instead.

<Warning>
  This is live: it changes what shoppers see at checkout. Leave it off at first and watch the scores on your orders for a few days before turning it on.
</Warning>

## Suggested starting setup

1. **Full payment** with a small discount, first in the list, to encourage prepaid.
2. **Partial payment** (for example 10–20% upfront) for higher-value carts using **Cart range**.
3. **COD** last, with **Max items for COD** and pincode blocks for areas with high returns.
4. Turn on the **Return-risk engine** and watch it for a few days before greying out COD.
