RabbitPay replaces the Shopify checkout with a faster 1-click checkout that verifies shoppers by OTP, prefills their address, and offers prepaid, partial-payment and COD options. This guide takes you from install to your first live order.

## Before you start

You need:

- A Shopify store, and access to its Shopify admin as the store owner.
- An account with a supported payment gateway: **Razorpay**, **PayU** or **Cashfree**. See [Payments](/help/payments).

## Setup checklist

A store is ready to go live when all five of these are done. Our team does some of them with you during onboarding.

| Step | Who does it | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Owner and store details filled | RabbitPay team | Onboarding |
| Billing mode chosen | RabbitPay team, with you | Onboarding (see [Wallet & billing](/help/wallet-and-billing)) |
| Transaction fee set | RabbitPay team, as agreed with you | Onboarding |
| Payment gateway connected | You | **Settings → Payments** |
| RabbitPay checkout switched on | RabbitPay team, after the checks above | Onboarding |

<Note>
  RabbitPay checkout is switched on only after your payment gateway is connected. If your checkout isn't showing yet, check that **Settings → Payments** shows your gateway as **Enabled**, then contact support.
</Note>

## Step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the RabbitPay app">
    Install RabbitPay from the link our team shares with you and approve the permissions in Shopify. Once it's installed, our team starts your onboarding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the RabbitPay dashboard">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps** and open **RabbitPay**. You're signed in to the RabbitPay dashboard automatically as the store owner. You can also sign in directly with your email and password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your payment gateway">
    Go to **Settings → Payments**, click **Configure** on your gateway, and enter its keys. Full steps are in [Payments](/help/payments).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose which products use RabbitPay checkout">
    By default every product opens RabbitPay checkout. To limit it to some products or collections, go to **Settings → Checkout Type**. See [Checkout settings](/help/checkout-settings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your payment options">
    Go to **RTO Suite** to decide how shoppers can pay: full prepaid, partial payment upfront, or COD, and for which carts. See [RTO Suite](/help/rto-suite).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund your wallet (wallet billing only)">
    If you're on wallet billing, add balance from **Wallet → Add to balance** so OTPs and notifications keep going out. See [Wallet & billing](/help/wallet-and-billing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place a test order">
    Once our team switches RabbitPay checkout on, add a product to the cart on your store and check out. Confirm that the OTP arrives, the address is saved, and the order shows up in Shopify and in **Orders** in the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Finding your way around the dashboard

| Section | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Sales and checkout overview |
| **Wallet** | Balance, recharges, and what's been charged |
| **Billing & Statements** | Monthly statement of charges |
| **Logs** | Delivery logs for OTPs and notifications |
| **Orders**, **Abandoned Cart** | Orders placed through RabbitPay, and checkouts that weren't completed |
| **Customer Engagement**, **Upsell**, **Discounts**, **Gift Coupons** | Marketing and offers at checkout |
| **Customization** | How the checkout looks |
| **RTO Suite** | Payment rules and return-risk controls |
| **Settings** | Store details, payments, integrations, team access, checkout type |

## Need help?

If something doesn't look right, contact RabbitPay support from the chat in your dashboard and include your store's `.myshopify.com` domain.
