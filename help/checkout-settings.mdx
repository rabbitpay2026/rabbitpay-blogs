## Choose which products use RabbitPay checkout

Go to **Settings → Checkout Type**. The **Where RabbitPay checkout appears** card controls which products open RabbitPay checkout. Everything else uses your normal Shopify checkout.

| Option | What happens |
|---|---|
| **All products** (default) | Every cart opens RabbitPay checkout. |
| **Only selected products** | Only carts with at least one of the products or collections you pick open RabbitPay checkout. |
| **All except selected products** | Every product uses RabbitPay checkout except the products or collections you pick. |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a mode">
    Under **Products**, choose one of the three options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select products or collections">
    For the two "selected" options, click **Pick** next to **Products** or **Collections** and choose items from your Shopify catalogue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. Click **Discard** to go back to what was saved before.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Things to know**

- **Mixed carts open RabbitPay.** If the cart contains *any* selected product, the whole cart goes through RabbitPay checkout.
- **Collections stay up to date.** Products you add to a selected collection later are covered automatically.
- **Nothing selected means nothing shows.** If you choose **Only selected products** and pick nothing, RabbitPay checkout won't appear on any product. The page warns you when this happens.

## Checkout options

These options change what shoppers see at checkout. Our team sets them up with you during onboarding. To change one, contact support.

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| SMS OTP button / WhatsApp OTP button | Which channels shoppers can get their login OTP on |
| Send OTP on SMS and WhatsApp | Sends the OTP on both channels at once, so it arrives even if one is slow. Each message is billed in its own channel. |
| Remember signed-in customers | Returning shoppers skip the OTP on the same device |
| Always ask for the address | Shows address selection even when a saved address exists |
| Show UPI QR / Recommended UPI apps | How UPI is offered on the payment step |
| Price breakdown on payment options | Shows how much is paid now and on delivery for each option |
| Offers & rewards block / Promo banner | Shows available offers and a promo banner at checkout |
| Order notes | Lets shoppers add a note to the order |
| Collect GST number | Lets business buyers enter a GSTIN for the invoice |
| Optimized Buy Now | Faster checkout from the product page's Buy Now button |
| Skip courier shipping-rate lookup | Faster checkout by skipping the live courier rate lookup in Shopify |
| Google Analytics | Sends checkout events to your Google Analytics |

<Tip>
  To show or hide individual payment methods (for example UPI or cards) on the payment step, use **Customization**.
</Tip>
