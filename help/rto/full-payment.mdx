A **Full payment** rule is your prepaid option: the shopper pays 100% online.

<Frame caption="Pick Full payment as the Amount type">
  <img src="/images/rto/amount-full.png" alt="Full payment selected; amount fixed at 100%" />
</Frame>

### How do I set up a full payment option?

1. **RTO Suite → Add payment rule**.
2. Name it (e.g. "Pay online") and set **Amount type** to **Full payment**. The amount is fixed at 100%.
3. Optionally add a discount and choose **Payment methods**.
4. Click **Create rule**.

### How do I make customers pay everything online?

Keep only a **Full payment** rule enabled: switch off or delete your partial and COD rules. Or keep COD but limit it with pincode, phone or max-item rules.

### Can I give a discount only when customers pay in full?

Yes. In the Full payment rule, fill in **Discount code**, **Discount type** and **Discount value**. The discount applies only to shoppers who choose this option. See [Discounts](/help/rto/discounts).

### How do I make full payment the first option?

Move the rule to the top in **RTO Suite**, or set the order in **Customization → Payments → Payment method order**. You can also make it the **Recommended payment method** there.

### How do I turn off the full payment option?

Open the rule, switch **Rule enabled** off and save.

### Why are my customers not choosing full payment?

Give them a reason: a small prepaid discount usually helps. Show it in the rule name (e.g. "Pay online & save 5%") and place the option first.

### Can I make full payment the only option for some orders?

Yes. Use conditions on your **other** rules so they don't show for those orders. For example, give the COD rule a **Max cart total**, a pincode block list or **Max items for COD**. When those rules are filtered out, full payment is what's left.

### How do I show full payment only for certain carts?

Add conditions to the Full payment rule: **Cart range**, **Applies to** (products), **Collections**, pincodes or phone numbers.

### Can I choose which online payment methods show for full payment?

Yes, in the rule's **Payment methods** section: UPI, Cards, Wallets, NetBanking and EMI. All are on by default except EMI.

  <img src="/images/rto/payment-methods.png" alt="UPI, Cards, Wallets, NetBanking and EMI switches" />
</Frame>

### Why is full payment not showing at checkout?

Check that the rule is enabled, the cart meets its conditions, your payment gateway is connected and **Enabled** in **Settings → Payments**, and that you're testing in a fresh checkout tab.
