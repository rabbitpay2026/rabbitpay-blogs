Each rule has two phone lists under **Phone number restrictions**:

- **Enable phone number restrictions**: a **block list**. The rule is **hidden** for these numbers.
- **Enable phone number whitelisting**: an **allow list**. The rule shows **only** for these numbers.

</Frame>

Numbers are matched against the shopper's **verified (OTP) phone number** using the **last 10 digits**.

### Can I block COD for certain phone numbers?

Yes. In your **COD** rule, tick **Enable phone number restrictions** and add the numbers.

### How do I stop customers who keep returning orders?

Add their numbers to the COD rule's block list. They can still pay online or with an advance. The [Return-risk engine](/help/rto/risk-engine) can also flag risky customers automatically.

### How do I allow COD only for some phone numbers?

In the COD rule, tick **Enable phone number whitelisting** and list the numbers. Only they see COD.

### What is the difference between blocking and allowing numbers?

Blocking hides the rule for listed numbers; everyone else sees it. Allowing shows it **only** for listed numbers.

### How do I add phone numbers to a rule?

Tick the list and type numbers separated by commas or new lines. Save.

### Can I add many numbers at once?

Yes. Paste them separated by commas or new lines.

### How do I remove a number from the list?

Delete it from the box and save.

### Can I give special payment options to my regular customers?

Yes. Create a rule (e.g. a special discount or a COD option) and use **Enable phone number whitelisting** with your regular customers' numbers.

### Why is my phone number rule not working?

Check that the list is ticked and saved, that you're testing with the **verified login phone** (not just a number typed in the address), and that you're in a fresh checkout tab.

### In what format should I add phone numbers?

Any format works. Country code and spaces are optional, because only the last 10 digits are matched: `+91 98765 43210` and `9876543210` are the same.
