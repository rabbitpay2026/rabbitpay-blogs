A **COD** rule has a **COD availability** section that controls what happens when COD isn't allowed.

</Frame>

### How do I show COD as disabled instead of hiding it?

In your COD rule, turn on **Show COD as disabled when blocked**. When COD is blocked for a product or pincode, shoppers see it greyed out with a message.

### What is the difference between hiding COD and greying it out?

**Hidden**: the COD option doesn't appear at all. **Greyed out**: it's visible but can't be selected, with a short explanation, so shoppers understand why and pay online instead.

### Can I show a message when COD is blocked?

Yes. With **Show COD as disabled when blocked** on, write it in **Message shown on the disabled COD option**. The default is "Cash on Delivery isn't available for this item or in your area".

### Why is COD greyed out for my customers?

One of these:

- the cart is over **Max items for COD**;
- a product, collection or pincode blocks COD and **Show COD as disabled when blocked** is on;
- the **Return-risk engine** flagged the shopper and **Grey out COD for high-risk customers** is on. Shoppers then see "Cash on Delivery isn't available for this order. You can pay online to place it."

### How do I stop COD from being greyed out?

Fix the cause above: raise or clear **Max items for COD**, remove the product, collection or pincode block, or switch off the risk engine's grey-out. If you'd rather hide COD than grey it out, switch **Show COD as disabled when blocked** off.

### How do I limit the number of items for COD?

Enter a number in **Max items for COD**, e.g. 3.

### What does max items for COD mean?

It's the most items (total quantity, not different products) a COD order can have. Above it, COD is greyed out and only prepaid is available.

### Can I stop COD for big orders?

Yes. By **items**, use **Max items for COD**. By **value**, set a **Max cart total** on the COD rule.

### What happens when the cart has more items than the limit?

COD is shown greyed out with your limit message, and the shopper can pay online. In the message you can use `{max_quantity}` for the limit.

### How do I remove the item limit for COD?

Clear **Max items for COD** (blank = no limit) and save.

### Why does COD disappear when customers add more items?

The cart went over **Max items for COD**, or above the COD rule's **Max cart total**.

### Will customers still be able to pay online when COD is blocked?

Yes. Blocking COD never blocks the order; prepaid and partial options stay available.

### Can I show "COD not available above ₹X" instead of hiding COD?

Yes. In your COD rule:

1. Set **Max cart total** to X.
2. Turn on **Show COD as disabled when blocked**.
3. In **Message shown on the disabled COD option**, write e.g. "COD is available on orders up to ₹2,999. Please pay online."

Bigger carts then see COD greyed out with your message. The same works when COD is blocked by a product, collection or pincode.

### What message should I write when COD is greyed out?

Say why, and what to do instead. For example:

- Pincode: "Cash on Delivery isn't available in your area yet. Pay online to place your order."
- Order value: "COD is available on orders up to ₹2,999. Please pay online."
- Product: "This item can only be bought with online payment."

One message is used for every reason, so if your COD rule has several conditions, keep it general.

### Does greying out COD convert better than hiding it?

Greying out tells shoppers COD exists but isn't available for **this** order, and why. Hidden COD can make a shopper think the store doesn't offer it, or that something is broken. If you're unsure, start with greyed out and a clear message, and compare your prepaid share in **Logs → Order Logs**.
