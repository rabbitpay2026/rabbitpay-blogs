The **Return-risk engine** (top of **RTO Suite**) scores every checkout for return-to-origin (RTO) risk, so you can see risky orders and optionally stop COD for them.

<Frame caption="Return-risk engine card in RTO Suite">
  <img src="/images/rto/risk-engine.png" alt="Engine on/off switch and Grey out COD for high-risk customers switch highlighted" />
</Frame>

### What is the return risk engine?

A scoring service that rates each checkout's chance of being refused or returned, and shows that score on your orders and abandoned carts.

### How do I turn on the return risk engine?

Go to **RTO Suite** and switch on the **Return-risk engine** card. It shows **Active** when on.

### How do I turn it off?

Switch it off on the same card.

### Why is the return risk engine off?

It's **off by default** for every store. Switch it on when you're ready.

### How does it decide if an order is risky?

It scores each checkout from the customer's **pincode and order history**, along with their phone, email and cart value.

### What does the risk score mean?

A score out of **100**: how likely the order is to be refused or returned. Orders are labelled **Low risk**, **Medium risk** or **High risk**.

### Does it look at the customer's pincode?

Yes.

### Does it look at the customer's past orders?

Yes. Order history is one of its main signals.

### Does it block orders by itself?

No. It never blocks an order. At most it can **grey out COD** for high-risk shoppers, and only if you turn that on. If the engine is unavailable, COD shows as normal.

### Can I see the reason behind a risk score?

Yes. Hover the risk badge in **Orders** or **Abandoned Cart**, or open an order and see the **Return risk** card with its reasons.

### Where do I see the risk score on my orders?

In **Orders**, the **RTO risk** column. Open an order for the full **Return risk** card.

### Does it also work on abandoned carts?

Yes. **Abandoned Cart** has an **RTO risk** column too, so you can prioritise follow-ups.

### Will turning it on change what my customers see at checkout?

No. Turning on the engine only adds scores. Checkout changes only if you also switch on **Grey out COD for high-risk customers**: then high-risk shoppers see COD greyed out with "Cash on Delivery isn't available for this order. You can pay online to place it."

### Can I use the risk engine and payment rules together?

Yes. They work side by side: your payment rules decide the options, and the engine can additionally grey out COD for high-risk shoppers.

### Is the risk engine free or do I pay extra?

It isn't listed as a separate charge in **Wallet** or **Billing & Statements**. If you're unsure about your plan, contact support.

### How can it help me reduce returns?

It tells you which orders are risky before you ship, so you can call to confirm, ask for prepayment, or let it grey out COD for high-risk shoppers automatically.

### What is RTO and why does it matter?

RTO (return to origin) is when a parcel comes back undelivered, usually a refused COD order. You pay shipping both ways and your stock is stuck in transit, so cutting RTO directly protects your margins.

### How accurate is the risk score?

It's a prediction, not a certainty. Turn the engine on, then **watch the scores on your orders for a few days** before you use them to grey out COD.

### What should I do with orders that show high risk?

Call or message the customer to confirm, ask them to prepay, or hold the order. If you see many, turn on **Grey out COD for high-risk customers**.

### How do I get started with the risk engine?

1. Switch the engine on in **RTO Suite**.
2. Watch the **RTO risk** column in **Orders** for a few days.
3. When you're comfortable, switch on **Grey out COD for high-risk customers**.
