You can charge a COD fee in two places:

- in a **COD** rule (the **COD fee** field next to Amount type);
- in a **partial payment** rule, under **Partial COD fee**.

<Frame caption="Partial COD fee: switch it on, then choose when it's paid">
  <img src="/images/rto/partial-cod-fee.png" alt="Include COD fee switch and the On delivery / Upfront options highlighted" />
</Frame>

### How do I add a COD fee?

- **COD rule:** open it, choose how the fee is calculated (**Fixed amount**, **Percentage of order value**, or **Whichever is higher**) and enter the amount.
- **Partial payment rule:** turn on **Include COD fee**, choose when it's collected, how it's calculated, and the amount.

### What does include COD fee mean?

It's the switch in **Partial COD fee** that adds a COD fee to a partial payment rule. Partial rules never borrow the fee from your COD rule; you set it here.

### Can customers pay the COD fee online instead of on delivery?

Yes, for partial payment rules. Under **Collect the COD fee**, choose **Upfront, with the advance**. The fee is added to the amount paid online now.

### How do I make the COD fee payable on delivery?

Choose **On delivery (default)**. The fee is added to the balance the courier collects. The total is the same either way.

### How do I make the COD fee payable upfront?

Choose **Upfront, with the advance** under **Collect the COD fee**.

### How do I remove the COD fee?

COD rule: clear the fee or set it to 0. Partial rule: switch **Include COD fee** off, or leave the amount blank or 0. Then save.

### Can I charge a COD fee on partial payment orders too?

Yes. That's what **Include COD fee** in the partial rule is for.

### What happens if I leave the COD fee empty?

No COD fee is added ("Blank or 0 = no COD fee").

### Why is the COD fee not showing for customers?

Check that the amount isn't blank or 0, that **Include COD fee** is on (partial rules), and that you saved. With **Percentage of order value**, the fee is worked out on the order total including tax and shipping, so very small orders may get a small fee. **Whichever is higher** charges the larger of your fixed amount and the percentage.

### How do I show the COD fee in the payment description?

In a partial rule's **Custom description (optional)**, insert `{cod_fee}`. For example: `Pay {pay_now} now, {pay_later} on delivery (incl. {cod_fee} COD fee)`.

### How do I charge a different COD fee for small and big orders?

Make one COD rule per price band, each with its own **Cart range** and **COD fee**. For example:

| Rule | Min cart total | Max cart total | COD fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| COD – small orders | 0 | 799 | ₹60 |
| COD – big orders | 799 | 2999 | ₹120 |

Only one COD rule is used per cart: the first matching one from the top of your list. See [Cart value limits](/help/rto/cart-value) for how to set the bands without gaps.

### How do I set up COD fee slabs, like ₹60 below ₹799 and ₹120 above?

Create two COD rules as in the table above. Give the second one **Max cart total** 0 if it should cover every order above ₹799. If you'd rather have one fee that grows with the order, use a single COD rule with **Percentage of order value**, or **Whichever is higher** (e.g. ₹60 or 2%).

### Should I write the COD fee in the rule name, or does it show automatically?

It shows automatically. The COD option at checkout is always titled **Cash on Delivery**, with the fee shown as "₹X COD fee added" and included in the price on the right. The COD rule's name is only for you; shoppers don't see it.

### Can I call the COD fee a "shipping charge" or "platform fee" at checkout?

Not on the COD option: it always says "COD fee". On a **partial payment** rule you can word it your way in **Custom description (optional)**, e.g. `Pay {pay_now} now, {pay_later} on delivery (incl. {cod_fee} handling)`.
