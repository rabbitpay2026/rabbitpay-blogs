The **Collections** section works like product restrictions, but uses your Shopify collections, so new products in a collection are covered automatically.

<Frame caption="Collections: pick a mode, then Select collections">
  <img src="/images/rto/collections.png" alt="Hide for selected option and Select collections button highlighted" />
</Frame>

| Option | Rule shows when… |
|---|---|
| **All collections** | always (no collection restriction) |
| **Only selected** | **every** cart product is in a selected collection |
| **Hide for selected** | **no** cart product is in a selected collection |

### Can I set payment rules by collection?

Yes, with the **Collections** section of any rule.

### How do I turn off COD for a whole collection?

In your **COD** rule, choose **Hide for selected** and select that collection. COD is hidden whenever a product from it is in the cart.

### How do I show a rule only for certain collections?

Choose **Only selected** and click **Select collections**.

### How do I hide a rule for certain collections?

Choose **Hide for selected** and click **Select collections**.

### How do I add collections to a rule?

Choose **Only selected** or **Hide for selected**, click **Select collections**, pick them and save. Their IDs appear in **Collection IDs**.

### What happens if the cart has items from different collections?

- **Only selected**: every product must be in one of your collections, unless **Match any product in cart** is ticked (then one is enough).
- **Hide for selected**: one product from a selected collection hides the rule.

### Why is my collection rule not working?

With **Only selected**, check whether another cart product sits outside your collections. Tick **Match any product in cart** if one match should be enough. Collection membership is cached for up to about 10 minutes, so very recent collection changes may take a few minutes to apply.

### What does hide for selected collections mean?

The rule is hidden if any product in the cart belongs to one of the selected collections.

### Do I have to select every product or can I just select the collection?

Just select the collection. Every product in it is covered, including ones you add later.

### Can I use product and collection rules together?

Yes. A cart must pass **both**: the **Applies to** (products) check and the **Collections** check.

### Can I exclude a collection and still use a cart-value limit on the same rule?

Yes. All conditions on a rule apply together. For example, a COD rule with **Hide for selected** (your premium collection) and **Max cart total** 2999 shows COD only when the cart has nothing from that collection **and** is ₹2,999 or less.
