<Warning>
  **Once you create or change a rule, always open your store in a new tab to check it** (a new incognito window is best). Checkout keeps the rules it loaded for as long as a browser tab stays open, so a tab that was already open won't show your latest changes.
</Warning>

### How do I check a rule after creating it?

1. Click **Create rule** (or **Save changes**).
2. Open your store in a **new tab**, ideally a new **incognito window**. Don't reuse a tab that already had checkout open.
3. Add a product that matches the rule's conditions (cart value, products, pincode and so on) and open checkout.
4. If you change the rule again, close that tab and open a new one before checking again.

### My payment rule is not showing at checkout, what should I check?

1. **Rule enabled** is on, and you saved.
2. **Cart range**: the cart total before discounts, without shipping or tax, is inside Min–Max.
3. **Applies to / Collections**: with **Only selected**, every cart product must match unless **Match any product in cart** is ticked.
4. **Pincode / phone lists**: they need the address or verified phone, and allow lists hide the rule for everyone else.
5. Only the **first** matching Full payment rule and the **first** matching COD rule are used.
6. Test in a **fresh tab**.

### Customers are seeing the wrong payment option, why?

Check your rule order, **Customization → Payments → Payment method order** (a custom order overrides rule order), and whether a higher rule of the same type is matching first.

### COD is showing even after I blocked it, why?

- Pincode blocks apply only once the address is entered.
- Phone blocks match the **verified OTP number** (last 10 digits).
- You may have a **second COD rule** without the block. Only the first matching COD rule is used, so if the first is blocked a lower one can take over.
- The checkout tab was open before your change.

### COD is not showing even though I turned it on, why?

Check the COD rule's cart range, product, collection, pincode and phone conditions, and **Max items for COD**. Also check the Return-risk engine's **Grey out COD for high-risk customers**.

### My discount is not applying on the rule, what's wrong?

Both **Discount code** and **Discount value** must be filled in. The discount applies only when the shopper picks that option. For COD, check the **Applicable discounts on COD** switches. See [Discounts](/help/rto/discounts).

### Shipping is not being added to the advance, why?

Switch on **Collect shipping charge upfront** in the partial rule. It exists only on Percentage and Fixed amount rules. See [Shipping charges](/help/rto/shipping).

### My pincode block is not working, what do I do?

Make sure the list is ticked and saved, ranges have the same number of digits on both sides, and the shopper has entered the address. Remember that a ticked but empty **allow list** restricts nothing.

### My phone number block is not working, what do I do?

Use the shopper's **verified login number**. Formatting doesn't matter, because only the last 10 digits are compared. Make sure the list is ticked and saved.

### I made changes but checkout still shows the old options, why?

The checkout tab loaded your rules earlier and keeps them while it stays open. Open the store in a **new incognito window** or a new browser session.

### Two rules are showing together, is this normal?

Yes, for **partial payment** rules: every matching one is offered as its own option. If you want only one, add cart range or other conditions so they don't overlap, or switch one off.

### No payment option is showing for my customers, what do I do?

Your rules' conditions may exclude that cart entirely. Keep at least one rule (usually Full payment) with no restrictions. Also check that your gateway is connected and **Enabled** in **Settings → Payments**.

### Customers say they can't pay the advance, what should I check?

Check **Settings → Payments**: the gateway must be **Enabled**, with live keys and the right environment (Production for live keys). See [Connect your payment gateway](/help/payments).

### Customers paid the advance but the order shows as COD, is that right?

Yes, that's expected. A partial order is created as **partially paid**: the advance is recorded as paid, and the rest is the COD balance. Open the order in **Orders** to see **Balance due (COD)**. The Shopify order note shows the amount paid online and the amount to collect.

### Why do some customers see COD and others don't?

Your COD rule's conditions differ per shopper: cart value, products, pincode, phone, item count, or the Return-risk engine greying it out for high-risk shoppers.

### My product restriction is not working, why?

With **Only selected**, every product in the cart must be in your list. Tick **Match any product in cart** if one match should be enough. With **All except selected**, one listed product hides the rule.

### My collection restriction is not working, why?

Same logic as products. Also, collection changes can take up to about 10 minutes to be picked up.

### The cart limit is not working the way I expected, why?

Cart range uses the total **before discounts, without shipping or tax**, and both limits are inclusive.

### How do I contact support if my rules are not working?

Click **Talk to a person** in the **RabbitAI** panel, or use the chat bubble in your dashboard. Include your store domain and the rule name.

### If my rule has products, cart value, pincodes and phone numbers, do all of them need to match?

Yes. A rule shows only when the cart passes **every** condition you've set: cart range **and** products **and** collections **and** pincode lists **and** phone lists. One failed condition hides the rule.

### My rule has many conditions and isn't showing. How do I find which one is blocking it?

Test one condition at a time:

1. Note down the rule's settings.
2. Untick or clear one condition (e.g. the pincode list), save, and check in a **new incognito window**.
3. If the rule now shows, that condition was blocking it. If not, put it back and try the next one.

Start with the usual suspects: **Cart range** (checked before discounts, without shipping or tax), **Only selected** products or collections, and allow lists, which hide the rule for everyone not on them.

### How can I test which rule a cart will get, without placing an order?

There's no rule simulator yet. Open your store in a **new incognito window**, add products to reach the cart value you want, go to checkout and enter a test phone and an address with the pincode you want to check. The payment screen shows exactly which options that cart gets; close the tab without paying.

### Can I see all my rules' cart ranges side by side to spot overlaps and gaps?

Not yet: the rules list doesn't show cart ranges. Open each rule to check its **Min cart total** and **Max cart total**. A simple habit helps: put the range in the name of COD and Full payment rules (shoppers don't see those names), e.g. "COD – ₹0 to ₹799".

### Why do I see two payment options for one cart value?

Two **partial payment** rules match that cart. Every matching partial rule is shown, so their cart ranges overlap. Start the next rule's **Min cart total** one paisa after the previous rule's **Max cart total** (e.g. 1999 → 1999.01). COD and Full payment rules never show twice; only the first match is used.
