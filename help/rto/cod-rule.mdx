COD at checkout comes from a payment rule whose **Amount type** is **COD**.

<Frame caption="A COD rule: the second field is the COD fee">

</Frame>

### How do I turn on COD for my store?

Create a rule with **Amount type** = **COD** (**RTO Suite → Add payment rule**), name it e.g. "Cash on Delivery", and keep **Rule enabled** on.

### How do I turn off COD?

Open your COD rule, switch **Rule enabled** off and save. Shoppers then see only online and partial options.

### Why is COD not showing to my customers?

Check that:

- the COD rule is enabled;
- the cart meets its conditions (cart range, products, collections, pincode and phone lists);
- the cart isn't over **Max items for COD**;
- the **Return-risk engine**'s "Grey out COD for high-risk customers" isn't greying it out for that shopper;
- you're testing in a fresh checkout tab.

### How do I add a COD rule?

**RTO Suite → Add payment rule** → **Amount type: COD** → set an optional COD fee → **Create rule**.

### Can I charge a fee for COD?

Yes. In a COD rule the second field is **COD fee**. Choose **Fixed amount**, **Percentage of order value** or **Whichever is higher**, then enter the amount. See [COD fee](/help/rto/cod-fee).

### How do I make COD free?

Leave the COD fee blank or set it to 0 ("Blank or 0 = no COD fee").

### How do I change the COD fee?

Edit the COD rule, change the fee and click **Save changes**.

### Where do I set the COD fee?

In the COD rule, next to **Amount type**. For partial payment rules it's the **Partial COD fee** section.

### Can I stop COD for some customers only?

Yes. In the COD rule, use **Phone number restrictions** to hide it for listed numbers, or **Zipcode restrictions** for areas. The Return-risk engine can also grey out COD for high-risk shoppers.

### Why is COD showing on orders where I don't want it?

Your COD rule has no condition that excludes those orders. Add a **Max cart total**, **Applies to → All except selected** (products), **Collections → Hide for selected**, pincode or phone block lists, or **Max items for COD**.

### Can I keep COD but ask for some payment online first?

Yes. That's a partial payment rule (Percentage or Fixed amount), which shows next to COD. To make an advance mandatory in some cases, hide the COD rule there and keep the partial rule. See [Partial payment](/help/rto/partial-payment).

### How do I show COD only for certain orders?

Add conditions to the COD rule: cart range, products, collections, **Enable zipcode whitelisting**, or **Enable phone number whitelisting**.

### If I don't create a COD rule, will customers see Cash on Delivery?

No. Cash on Delivery appears only when you have an enabled COD rule that matches the cart. Without one, shoppers see only your online and partial payment options.

### How do I make my store prepaid-only?

Switch off (or delete) your COD rule, and your partial payment rules too if you don't want advances. Keep a **Full payment** rule enabled with no restrictions, so every shopper can pay online. See [Full payment](/help/rto/full-payment).

### How do I switch COD back on after running prepaid-only?

Open your COD rule and switch **Rule enabled** back on (or create one with **Amount type: COD** if you deleted it). Save, then check in a new incognito window.
