With **partial payment** the shopper pays an advance online and the rest on delivery. Shoppers who have paid something are much more likely to accept the parcel.

<Frame caption="A Percentage rule: the Amount is the advance">
  <img src="/images/rto/amount-percentage.png" alt="Amount type Percentage and Amount field highlighted" />
</Frame>

### How does partial payment work?

You create a **Percentage** or **Fixed amount** rule. At checkout it appears as an option; the shopper pays the advance online through your gateway, and the order is created in Shopify as **partially paid** with the balance due on delivery.

### How do I ask customers to pay some amount now and the rest on delivery?

1. **RTO Suite → Add payment rule**.
2. Name it, e.g. "Pay 20% now, rest on delivery".
3. **Amount type**: **Percentage** (enter 20) or **Fixed amount** (enter e.g. 199).
4. Click **Create rule**.

### How do I set the advance amount?

It's the **Amount** field of the rule: a percentage for Percentage rules, rupees for Fixed amount rules.

### Can I change how much customers pay in advance?

Yes. Edit the rule, change **Amount** and save.

### Can I take a percentage as advance instead of a fixed amount?

Yes. Set **Amount type** to **Percentage**.

### Will partial payment actually reduce my returns?

It usually does, because shoppers who have paid an advance are far less likely to refuse delivery. Watch your RTO in **Logs → Order Logs** (it splits Prepaid, COD and Partial) and the **RTO risk** column in **Orders**.

### How do I stop asking for advance payment?

Switch **Rule enabled** off on the partial rule (or delete it).

### Can I use partial payment only on some orders?

Yes. Add conditions: **Cart range**, **Applies to** (products), **Collections**, pincode or phone lists.

### How do I show partial payment only for risky areas?

Use **Enable zipcode whitelisting** on the partial rule and list the risky pincodes. Pair it with a COD rule that blocks those same pincodes, so shoppers there must pay an advance. See [Pincode restrictions](/help/rto/pincodes).

### What does the customer see for the remaining amount?

By default the option shows how much is paid now and how much on delivery. You can write your own wording in **Custom description (optional)**.

### Can I change the text customers see for pay now and pay later?

Yes. Use **Custom description (optional)** in the rule (Percentage and Fixed amount rules only). Each line shows on its own line.

### How do I show pay now and pay later amounts in the description?

Click the chips under the description box to insert live amounts: `{pay_now}`, `{pay_later}`, `{total}`, `{item_total}`, `{discount}`, `{shipping}`, `{tax}`, `{cod_fee}`. For example: `Pay {pay_now} now, {pay_later} on delivery`.

### Why is partial payment not showing at checkout?

Check **Rule enabled**, the rule's conditions (cart range, products, collections, pincode, phone), that your payment gateway is connected and enabled, and that you're testing in a fresh checkout tab.

### How do I find out how much advance a customer paid?

In **Orders**, the **Amount** column shows what was paid, with "of ₹total" under it when it's a part payment. Open the order to see the paid transaction. In Shopify, the order note shows "Paid online: ₹X".

### Where can I see the remaining amount for an order?

Open the order in **Orders**: the Payment card shows **Balance due (COD)**. In Shopify, the order note shows "Collect on delivery: ₹Y".
