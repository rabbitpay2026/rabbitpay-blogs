A **payment rule** is one payment option at checkout, such as "Pay 20% now, rest on delivery" or "Cash on Delivery". You manage them all in **RTO Suite → Payment rules**.

### How do I add a new payment rule?

1. Go to **RTO Suite** and click **Add payment rule**.
2. Enter a **Rule name** and pick the **Amount type** (Percentage, Fixed amount, Full payment or COD).
3. Set the amount and any conditions you need (cart range, products, pincodes and so on).
4. Keep **Rule enabled** on and click **Create rule**.

<Frame caption="Bottom of the rule form">
  <img src="/images/rto/rule-enabled-save.png" alt="Rule enabled switch and Create rule button highlighted" />
</Frame>

### How do I edit a rule I already made?

Click the **pencil** icon on the rule's row (or click its name), change what you need, then click **Save changes**.

### How do I delete a rule?

Click the red **trash** icon on the rule's row, then click **Delete** in the confirmation box. Checkout stops offering that option straight away.

<Frame caption="Delete confirmation">
  <img src="/images/rto/rto-delete-dialog.png" alt="Delete this rule? confirmation dialog" />
</Frame>

### How do I turn a rule off without deleting it?

Open the rule, switch **Rule enabled** off and click **Save changes**. The rule stays in your list but shoppers don't see it.

### I turned off a rule, is it gone for good?

No. "Off" means **saved but hidden at checkout**. Switch **Rule enabled** back on whenever you want it again. Only **Delete** removes a rule permanently.

### How do I rename a rule?

Open the rule, change the **Rule name** field and click **Save changes**.

### What name should I give a rule, will customers see it?

It depends on the rule type:

- **Percentage** and **Fixed amount** (partial payment) rules: yes, the rule name is the option's title at checkout. Use shopper-friendly wording, e.g. "Pay 10% now & rest COD".
- **COD** rules: no. The option is always titled **Cash on Delivery**; the name is only for you.
- **Full payment** rules: no. Shoppers see the online methods (Pay via UPI, Cards, and so on), with your discount shown next to them.

For COD and Full payment rules, pick a name that helps **you**, e.g. "COD – orders up to ₹799".

### Can I make more than one rule?

Yes. Most stores have a few: for example a full payment rule, one or two partial payment rules and a COD rule.

### Is there a limit on how many rules I can have?

No, there's no limit.

### How do I find a rule quickly?

Type part of its name in **Search payment rules…** above the list.

### I saved my rule but customers can't see it, why?

Check these in order:

1. **Rule enabled** is on.
2. The cart matches the rule's conditions: **cart range**, **products**, **collections**, **pincode** and **phone** lists.
3. Only the **first** matching **Full payment** rule and the **first** matching **COD** rule are used. A second one of the same type won't show.
4. The shopper started a **fresh checkout**. An already-open checkout tab keeps the rules it loaded, so test in a new tab or incognito window.

See [Troubleshooting](/help/rto/troubleshooting) for more.

### Why is my new rule not showing at checkout?

Same checks as above. The most common causes are a cart range the test cart doesn't meet, and testing in a checkout tab that was already open before you saved.

### Do I have to save every time I change something?

Yes. Changes only apply after you click **Create rule** or **Save changes**. If you leave with unsaved edits, a bar at the top warns you and offers **Discard** or save.

### Can I copy an existing rule to make a new one?

There's no copy button yet. Click **Add payment rule** and enter the same settings again.

### Is it better to make one rule with many conditions or several simple rules?

It depends on the rule type:

- **COD**: only one COD rule is used per cart (the first match), so put your blocks (pincodes, phones, collections, item limit) on **one** COD rule. Use several COD rules only for different fees by cart value, and repeat the blocks on each.
- **Partial payment**: several simple rules are fine; each matching one shows as its own option.
- **Full payment**: one rule is usually enough. Add more only for different discounts or payment methods by cart value.

### Which text does the customer see: the rule name, the heading or the description?

For **partial payment** rules: the **Rule name** is the title, and **Custom description (optional)** shows underneath (e.g. "Pay ₹99 now, ₹700 on delivery"). For **COD**, shoppers see "Cash on Delivery" with the COD fee; for **Full payment**, they see the online methods with your discount. See the question above about rule names.
