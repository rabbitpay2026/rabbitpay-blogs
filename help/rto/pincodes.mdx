Each rule has two pincode lists under **Zipcode restrictions**:

- **Enable zipcode restrictions**: a **block list**. The rule is **hidden** for these pincodes.
- **Enable zipcode whitelisting**: an **allow list**. The rule shows **only** for these pincodes.

<Note>Pincode lists are checked once the shopper enters a delivery address. Until then the rule isn't hidden.</Note>

### How do I block COD for certain pincodes?

In your **COD** rule, tick **Enable zipcode restrictions** and enter the pincodes under **Zipcodes to hide this rule**. Save.

### How do I allow COD only in some areas?

In your COD rule, tick **Enable zipcode whitelisting** and list the allowed pincodes. Everyone else won't see COD.

### How do I stop COD in areas with too many returns?

Add those pincodes to the COD rule's block list. You can also turn on the [Return-risk engine](/help/rto/risk-engine), which scores pincodes automatically.

### What is the difference between blocking and allowing pincodes?

Blocking hides the rule for the listed pincodes; everyone else sees it. Allowing shows the rule **only** for the listed pincodes; everyone else doesn't.

### How do I add pincodes to a rule?

Tick the list you want and type pincodes separated by **commas or new lines**.

### Can I add many pincodes at once?

Yes. Paste them separated by commas or new lines. You can also use **ranges** like `400001-400010` (both ends must have the same number of digits).

### How do I remove a pincode from the list?

Delete it from the box and save. To stop using the list completely, untick it.

### How do I show a rule only for some locations?

Use **Enable zipcode whitelisting** on that rule.

### How do I hide a rule for some locations?

Use **Enable zipcode restrictions** on that rule.

### Can I ask for advance payment only in some areas?

Yes. Give a partial payment rule an **allow list** of those pincodes, and give the COD rule a **block list** with the same pincodes. Shoppers there must pay an advance; others still get COD.

### Why is my pincode rule not working?

Check that:

- the list is ticked and saved;
- the shopper has entered their address (lists are checked only then);
- ranges have the same number of digits on both sides;
- an **allow list** that's ticked but empty restricts nothing;
- you're testing in a fresh checkout tab.

### Can I use both blocking and allowing at the same time?

Yes. A pincode must then be **on the allow list and not on the block list** for the rule to show.
