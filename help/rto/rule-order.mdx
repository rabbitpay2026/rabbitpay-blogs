Rules are listed top to bottom in **RTO Suite → Payment rules**, and checkout reads them in that order.

### What does rule order mean?

It's the position of each rule in your list. Checkout goes through your rules from the top, and the order of the payment options shoppers see follows it.

### How does the order of rules work?

- **Partial payment rules** (Percentage or Fixed amount): every enabled rule that matches the cart is shown as its own option, in your order.
- **Full payment rules**: only the **first** matching one is used. It decides the prepaid options (UPI, cards and so on) and any prepaid discount.
- **COD rules**: only the **first** matching one is used.

### Which rule shows first at checkout?

By default, options follow your rule order. Two settings can change that:

- **Customization → Payments → Payment method order**: if you set a custom order there, it wins.
- If the UPI QR option is on and you haven't set a custom order, UPI is shown at the top.

### What happens if two rules match the same order?

Two matching **partial** rules both show, as two options. Two matching **Full payment** rules, or two matching **COD** rules, don't both show: only the higher one in the list is used.

### How do I change the order of my rules?

Click the **up** or **down** arrow on a rule's row. The new order saves straight away and you'll see "Rule order updated".

### How do I make one rule my default?

Move it to the top of the list. The option shoppers find already selected is your **recommended method** (set in **Customization → Payments**) if you've chosen one; otherwise it's the first option that isn't a partial payment.

### Why is the wrong rule showing first?

Check **Customization → Payments → Payment method order**. A custom order there overrides your rule order. Also check that the rule you expect isn't switched off or excluded by its conditions.

### Does the top rule always win?

Only among rules of the same kind: the top **Full payment** rule and the top **COD** rule are the ones used. Partial payment rules don't compete; every matching one is shown.

### I moved a rule up, why is nothing changing?

Either a custom **Payment method order** in Customization is overriding it, or you're looking at a checkout tab that was open before the change. Open the store in a new tab or incognito window.

### How should I arrange my rules?

A good starting order:

1. **Full payment** (with a small discount to encourage prepaid)
2. **Partial payment** rules, most important first
3. **COD** last
