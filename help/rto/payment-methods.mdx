Which online methods shoppers can use is set in your **Full payment** rule's **Payment methods** section. The order they appear in is set in **Customization**.

<Frame caption="Payment methods in a Full payment rule">
  <img src="/images/rto/payment-methods.png" alt="UPI, Cards, Wallets, NetBanking and EMI switches; EMI off" />
</Frame>

### How do I change the online payment options my customers see?

Open your **Full payment** rule → **Payment methods**, turn methods on or off, and save.

### How do I hide a payment method?

Switch it off in the Full payment rule's **Payment methods** and save.

### How do I show only UPI?

In **Payment methods**, keep **UPI** on and switch off **Cards**, **Wallets**, **NetBanking** and **EMI**.

### How do I turn off cards?

Switch off **Cards** in the Full payment rule.

### How do I turn off EMI?

EMI is **off by default**. If you turned it on, switch **EMI** off again.

### How do I turn on wallets?

Switch **Wallets** on (it's on by default).

### Can I remove netbanking?

Yes. Switch **NetBanking** off.

### Are all payment methods on by default?

UPI, Cards, Wallets and NetBanking are on by default. EMI is off.

### Can I pick different payment methods for different rules?

No. Only the **first matching Full payment rule** decides the methods. You can create several Full payment rules with different conditions (say, different cart ranges); whichever matches first controls the methods for that cart.

### Why is a payment method not showing for my customers?

It may be switched off in the Full payment rule, your gateway may not support it, or the gateway isn't **Enabled** in **Settings → Payments**. Check the order too: **Customization → Payments → Payment method order**.

### Where do I choose which prepaid options show?

In **RTO Suite**, in your Full payment rule → **Payment methods**. The order is set in **Customization → Payments → Payment method order**.

### Can I allow only some payment methods for small orders?

Yes. Make two Full payment rules with different **Cart range** values and different **Payment methods**. Put the small-order rule first; it applies to carts in its range.
