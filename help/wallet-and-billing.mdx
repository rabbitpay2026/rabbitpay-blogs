## What you're charged for

Your rates are agreed with you during onboarding. You can see every charge in **Wallet → Billing charges** and in **Billing & Statements**.

| Charge | When it applies |
|---|---|
| **Transaction fee** | A percentage of the order amount on every prepaid order **and** every partial-payment order. For partial payments it's charged on the full order amount, not just the part paid upfront. |
| **COD transaction fee** | A percentage of the order amount on every COD order |
| **Pre-order OTP** (SMS / WhatsApp) | Per OTP message sent at checkout |
| **SMS**, **WhatsApp utility**, **WhatsApp marketing**, **IVR** | Per message or call sent to your customers |
| **Abandoned cart** (SMS / WhatsApp) | Per recovery message sent |

Each order is charged either the transaction fee or the COD fee, never both. If you joined through a RabbitPay partner, the partner's agreed fee is added on top of RabbitPay's.

<Note>
  If you turn on **Send OTP on SMS and WhatsApp**, each OTP is sent twice, and each message is billed at its own channel's rate.
</Note>

### Fees returned on cancellations and refunds

If an order is cancelled or its payment is refunded, the transaction fee for that order is returned to your wallet. You can see these under **Wallet → Refund credits**.

## Billing modes

Your store uses one of three billing modes, set up with you during onboarding:

| Mode | How you pay |
|---|---|
| **Wallet** (prepaid) | You add balance in advance. Charges are deducted from it automatically every hour. |
| **Shopify usage charges** | Charges are added to your Shopify bill as app usage charges. |
| **Postpaid via COD King** | For stores that also use COD King: usage is billed daily to your COD King Shopify bill. |

## Recharge your wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Wallet">
    Go to **Wallet** in the dashboard. **Current Balance** shows what's left.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an amount">
    Under **Add to balance**, enter the amount in ₹. The minimum recharge is **₹500**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the total">
    GST is added on top. You'll see the **Recharge (added to balance)**, the **GST**, and the **Total payable**. Only the recharge amount is added to your balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Pay securely through Razorpay. Your balance updates as soon as the payment clears. If you see "Payment received — balance will update shortly", it will appear within a few minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every recharge has a **GST invoice**, which you can download from the **Top-ups** list.

## When your balance runs low

- **Overdraft buffer.** Your wallet can go a little below zero (shown as **Overdraft buffer**) so a short shortfall doesn't interrupt anything.
- **Floor.** If your balance drops below the **Floor**, you get a low-balance alert, and messages such as notifications **pause until you recharge**.
- **Charges still post.** Usage that has already happened is always recorded, so your balance can go negative. It's settled from your next recharge.

<Warning>
  Recharge before you reach the floor to avoid paused messages during busy sales.
</Warning>

## Postpaid stores

If your store moved from wallet to postpaid billing, **Wallet** shows the balance you brought over and how it's being used:

- Any credit pays your daily bills first.
- Any amount owed is added to your next COD King Shopify bill.
- This balance can't be topped up in postpaid mode.

## Statements and exports

- **Wallet → Billing charges**: each charge, with filters for date range (Today, 7d, 30d, This month) and category. Open a charge to see the records behind it.
- **Billing & Statements**: your charges for a chosen **Statement month**.
- Lists can be exported for your accounts team.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I paid but my balance didn't go up">
    Wait a few minutes and refresh the page. Payments are confirmed by Razorpay in the background. If it still hasn't updated after 15 minutes, contact support with your Razorpay payment ID.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why was I charged a fee on a partial-payment order?">
    Partial-payment orders are billed at the transaction fee on the full order amount, the same as prepaid orders.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An order was cancelled. Do I get the fee back?">
    Yes. The fee is returned to your wallet and shows under **Refund credits**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
